What we are asking for

Mentors

Time with teams during the event — sense-checking an idea, pointing at the obstacle they have not seen yet, and being honest about what would happen in a real clinical setting.

Judges

Assessing the final pitches against innovation, feasibility, clinical relevance and equity.

  • Engineers, designers and product people
  • Policy, health economics and implementation
  • Founders, operators and investors
